Founded in 2017

Elite Property Management Inc. is a South Florida renovation, remodeling and property-maintenance company built around one simple idea: give property owners one reliable point of contact for getting things done correctly.

Our core work is residential remodeling and renovation, including kitchens, bathrooms, interior renovations, windows and doors, painting, exterior improvements, landscaping and irrigation repairs. We also work with newly purchased properties, helping owners take a home from its current condition through a complete renovation, upgrade or repair plan.

For seasonal residents and traveling homeowners, we provide scheduled property check-ins, maintenance coordination and project oversight while they are away. That can mean identifying an issue before it becomes a larger problem, coordinating a repair, or managing renovation work in the owner’s absence. We also provide maintenance support for commercial properties.

Owner Joseph Hansen brings nearly three decades of hands-on construction field experience. Rather than managing from behind a desk, that experience has been built day after day in the field — from rough construction through detailed finish work. That practical background allows Elite to evaluate problems quickly, understand how different trades work together, and keep projects moving efficiently.

We are not a traditional rental-management company. We do not collect rent, screen tenants or manage leases. Our focus is the physical property itself.

To support that, Elite coordinates a trusted network of trades and service professionals, including cleaning crews, electricians and plumbers. We manage the work, assign the right people to the right tasks and give the owner a single point of contact instead of requiring them to coordinate multiple contractors on their own.

From a small repair to a full-home renovation, the goal is the same: solve the problem, improve the property and make life easier for those who are not on site every day.
