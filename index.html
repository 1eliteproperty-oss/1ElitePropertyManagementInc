We Manage the Property — Not the Tenants.

Elite Property Management Inc. manages the physical needs of your home, vacation property, Airbnb or investment property as a whole — from watching over the property while you are away, to maintenance, repairs, improvements and full renovations.

What we handle: property oversight while owners are away, Airbnb and vacation-home maintenance support, repair coordination, general maintenance, kitchens, bathrooms, tile, flooring, cabinetry, carpentry, doors, windows, finish work and complete renovations.

What we do not do: collect rent, screen tenants, manage leases or provide traditional tenant-management services.

Founded in 2017

Elite Property Management Inc. was founded to give homeowners and property owners one reliable point of contact for the physical needs of their property. The company combines property oversight, maintenance, repair work and renovation under one roof.

With decades of hands-on construction experience — from rough construction through finish carpentry, tile and detailed interior work — Elite is built to solve problems, maintain properties and improve them over time.

Our approach is simple: manage the property itself. Whether you need someone keeping an eye on a home while you are away, handling an Airbnb maintenance issue, completing a repair or taking on a full renovation, we focus on the condition, function and quality of the property.
